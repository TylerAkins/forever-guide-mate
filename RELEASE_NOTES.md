## 0.3.0 - 2026-10-08

- **Forever Casual Route**: one Alliance and one Horde route from level 12 to 60, rebuilt from proven leveling paths with Forever quests woven in. The old `1-60 Era` guide and its separate 30-60 chapters are gone; your progress carries over.
- **Skip with care**: skipping a step also skips the steps that depend on it, after a confirm. Skips never count toward completion, and Sync clears them.
- **Smarter steps**: objectives wait until the quest is in your log, turn-ins wait until it is complete, and "Use the item" quests wait until the drop is in your bags. Rare drops get a kill-and-loot note with a map pin first.
- **Better pins**: turn-ins and objectives follow Blizzard's quest map pin, choosing the closest one when there are several. Guide coordinates are the fallback.
- **Smarter flights**: the guide names the right camp (for example Crossroads, Ratchet, or Camp Taurajo), remembers which flight master you used, and updates when you take off and land.
- **Tracker**: right-click the cog for settings. Steps read **Accept Quest** and **Turn in Quest**.
- **Smoother play**: looting and quest pickup no longer stutter, and the guide makes far less memory garbage over a long session.
- **Quest fixes**: The Spirits of Stonetalon at Zor Lonetree, The Escape escort, Miner's Fortune, Chen's Empty Keg, Return to the Crossroads, and Blackfathom Deeps drop chains.
- **Bug fixes**: no more Lua errors when opening the flight map or boarding a flight.
