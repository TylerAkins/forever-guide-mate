## 0.3.1 - 2026-10-08

- **Casual moves on right after login**: accepting a quest advances the Forever Casual Route right away. Before, the route could sit on the same step for a long time after a login or reload while the guide read quests from every other guide.
- **Mark complete works on quests you cannot get**: the check mark now moves past an accept the quest log says you never took, such as a trainer quest your race is not offered (issue #118). The step still does not count toward completion, Back reopens it, and it clears on its own if you accept the quest later.
- **Hunter taming quests**: only Human and Gnome hunters are sent to Josephine Carson in Goldshire. Night Elf, Dwarf, and Skyborne hunters keep their own race's Taming the Beast chain.
- **Class quests offered by several trainers**: once you take one trainer's version of a class quest, such as Heeding the Call, The Hunter's Path, or Summon Felsteed, the guide skips the others instead of sending you to each one.
- **Class quests for the right race**: class guides no longer send you to quests your race is not offered, or to follow-ups of a quest your race cannot do.
- **Class quest order**: the Skyborne hunter taming quests, the Horde warrior Brutal armor quests, the warlock Completed Orb quests, and the shaman Sapta quests now come after the quest that unlocks them.
- **Shaman Call of Earth**: Tauren shamans do the Mulgore rite, and Orc and Troll shamans do the Durotar rite. Finishing one skips the other.
- **Starter zone class quests**: the starting letters, sigils, and other class quests in the starter zones only show for the race and class that can take them. Night Elf warriors, rogues, priests, and hunters are now sent to their own sigil in Teldrassil.
- **Race-locked quests**: Tauren-only Mulgore quests, Rude Awakening, the Zephras Isle tours, and a few race-locked dungeon quests only show for the races that can take them.
